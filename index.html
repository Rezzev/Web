About RezzOS

RezzOS is built from scratch with a focus on simplicity, security, and a fully static userland. We use musl as the C standard library, BusyBox for core utilities, and runit for process supervision. Packages are managed via our custom statically-linked apk implementation.


Our goal is to provide a minimal, highly customizable operating system for developers and power users who want full control over their environment.
